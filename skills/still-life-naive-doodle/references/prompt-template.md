# Prompt Template

Use this reference when generating or editing the image. Replace bracketed details after inspecting the source.

## Main prompt

Reference the supplied still-life photo and convert it into a minimalist, deliberately naive hand-drawn illustration. Preserve the identity and relative placement of [main object 1], [main object 2], and [main object 3], but remove the background and all secondary detail. Do not trace the photograph literally.

Draw the objects with sparse, casual, uneven black pen lines. Use simple, slightly awkward shapes and intentionally imperfect proportions. Contours may wobble, break, tilt, overlap, or remain partly open, as if quickly doodled by hand on paper. Avoid accurate perspective and polished construction.

Use a pure warm-white or light-ivory background with about 80% untouched negative space. Keep the still-life group small and place it near the lower center or in one intentional corner. Show only the minimum tabletop or grounding line needed for readability; do not render a complete room.

Keep the image almost entirely black linework. Use only one small accent color, [accent color], on a selected detail such as fruit, a flower, a drink, a leaf, or packaging. No large filled areas. Create subtle, dry humor through the objects' arrangement: a cup leaning against a book, fruit forming an odd queue, a flower reaching too far, utensils crossing a plate, or objects stacked in an improbable but balanced way.

The result should feel quiet, loose, restrained, playful, and editorial: a simple everyday visual poem rather than a polished cartoon. Output one standalone illustration.
